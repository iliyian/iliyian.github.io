Jay Chou’s Songs

My absolute favorites, each bringing me immense happiness. I hope you, my esteemed friend, will find them to your liking. Ranked in order of preference.

You Can Hear It

Who could know better than I, that your tenderness is like a feather

Secretly lying in my embrace, only you can hear it

Does anyone else know, that your smile is like an embrace

How I wish to hide your goodness, for my eyes alone to see

Standing on the rooftop, telling only the wind, I don’t want to be swayed

I used to hate the rainy sky, until I heard someone say they love me

Sitting on the second floor of the cinema, watching the crowd pass by

Why did the two of us that day, silently smile for so long

I think I am far too dependent, just now as we hung up the phone

Insisting on being like an innocent child, quietly guarding this love

Knowing I shouldn’t rely too much, afraid you might spoil me

Your fragrance lingers on and on, I cannot bear to leave

I’m Not Worthy

This street is too crowded, too many people hold secrets

Mist upon the glass, whose past is being hidden away

The emotion on your face, is recreating that rain

This alley is too winding, I can’t walk back into the story

These days are no longer green, mottled with a few more words

Leaving me, hollowed of memories, in this big house

The cinema seats, separate us by a vast distance

Love has no co-star here, you are playing chess with yourself

Before I could even, carefully, write down everything about you

Describing how I love you, you left me with a smile

This feeling is already wrong, I’m trying hard to redeem it

Those little moments of tenderness I should have given, I failed to provide

The wish you made with a pout was so humble, compromising

It was my neglect, you merely wanted someone by your side

This feeling is already wrong, only at the end did I understand

Page after page of plots too painful to read, you are so tired

You silently memorized, how many tears you shed for me, how worn out you are

While my heart breaks and you suffer, your beauty, I am not worthy of

Rain in the Heart

Oh, the green leaves of the oak, the white bamboo fence

How I wish to tell my girl, that this place is like a painting

Last year’s Christmas card, the stubble in the mirror

She begins to fade from the frame, yet I’m still playing the fool

Promised to brew flower tea for me, learning to set the knives and forks

An empty home in the student dorm, guarding the phone yet waiting in vain for her

The rain in my heart pours down in torrents, yet it cannot wet her hair

Tears blur the longing on the postcard; turns out, heartbreak knows no time difference

The rain in my heart pours down in torrents, yet it can never drench her

The cold wind passes through the branches in the yard, chilling the fresh flowers in my hand

Dandelion’s Promise

The dandelions by the grade school fence, are a fragrant scenery in my memory

The sound of cicadas from the playground during nap time, still sounds so sweet after all these years

Folding wishes into paper planes to send as letters, because we couldn’t wait for that shooting star

(Standing in the hallway as punishment, palms smacked, yet our eyes were on the dragonfly by the window)

Earnestly tossing a coin to decide our fate, yet not knowing where we could truly go

(Wherever I went, you followed so closely; so many dreams were waiting to unfold)

The promise of growing up together, so crystal clear; we pinky-swore, and I believed

The promise to travel together, is now, the only whim you stubbornly hold onto

(The promise of growing up together, so sincere; the endless past we used to talk about)

(Yet I can no longer tell, whether you are friendship, or a love I missed)

Hair Like Snow

Beneath the crescent moon, my fair lady pines away; I raise my chalice, drinking down the wind and snow

Who hath overturned the cabinet of past lives, stirring up the dust of mortal strife

The incantation of fate, through cycles of samsara; thou knit’st thy brows, weeping for a beauty beyond recall

Though the annals of history be turned to ash, my love shall never die

Worldly splendor flows like three thousand eastward streams, yet I take but a single gourd of love to fathom

Enamored only of the butterfly thou hast become

Thy hair like snow, rendering our parting poignantly fair; I burn incense, yet whose heart doth it move

Inviting the bright moon, to purify our memories, love finds perfection 'neath the lunar glow

Thy hair like snow, scattering falling tears; my vigil hath aged whom in time

Drunk in the mortal realm, through tipsy fleeting years

With no regret, I carve a monument of eternal love for thee

(La-er-la, la-er-la, la-er-la-er-la)

(The bronze mirror reflects thy guileless face, tying a ponytail)

(Shouldst thou run wild, in this life I shall pledge thee with wine till the end)

Sorry

A copper coin in the plaza, its sorrow deeply hidden

In the wishing well, it softly sighs

Too many "I love you"s, leave it breathless

Having already lost their meaning

The ring is weeping, lying quietly in the drawer

All it possesses now are mere memories

Falling in love and parting ways, like rain that cannot be scheduled

Ready to strike at any moment

I miss the desks and chairs of grade school, miss writing diaries in pencil

Recording that most primal beauty, recording the very first time I met you

Jay Chou

If meeting you was a tragedy

I think I am destined in this life to act in a solo play

And in the end, slowly reminisce all alone

Stripped of the past, I detach myself from bygone days

If meeting you was a tragedy

I can let my life just be meaningless like this

Perhaps at the very end, I might hear a word from you

A gentle sigh, regretfully saying sorry

(If meeting you was a tragedy)

(I softly sigh, regretfully saying sorry)

Obviously

So many colors in the candy jar, yet the smile is no longer sweet

Some of your joys, happen in moments without me

In this medieval city, I think I’ll just walk up to here

When seagulls no longer yearn for the sea, they can fly much further

Bagpipes echo from afar, I only care for news of you

The castle guards secrets for love, while I guard memories for you

Obviously not used to holding hands, why then did you reach out to hook mine

You have too many weights on your mind, I won’t expose them

Obviously he is more gentle, perhaps he can give you more

No need to choose, I will automatically become just a friend

Handwritten Past

This wind chime, is so close to a fluttering heart

This letter, is still reminiscing about the journey

The loves that passed by, were all too young

You are the scenery I wish, to return to once more

This parting, is bottled up as a secret

This daisy, is as beautiful as a line of poetry

While I wait in the wind for news, of you

Waiting for moonlight to fall on snow, for maple red to dye the autumn, waiting to meet

I revisit the afternoon sunlight, slinging the guitar across my shoulder

Just like many years ago, we softly sing, going anywhere at all

I look at, your face, gently strumming the chords

On the Valentine’s card, a handwritten forever

(First love is an entire expanse, of the handwritten past)

Still remembering, the plaza park, performing together

(Still remembering, that autumn, when we said goodbye)

The candy shop by the campus, is faintly sweet in my memory

(When the romance has drifted far away, I bury you deep within my heart)

The breeze needs the bamboo grove, the stream needs the dragonfly

A departure like homesickness, needs drifting duckweed

Remembering the rainy season of that year, so remarkably quiet in my memory

The decision made after crying, can it still be carried on

I foolishly wait, foolishly waiting for spring to warm and flowers to bloom

Waiting till the end, till it’s clear and understood, waiting for love to return

Youth belongs to confessions, sunlight belongs to the windowsill

And I think I belong to, a future that holds you

A rainbow on paper, a clock drawn in sketch

I am still revising, your smile within my memories

How should I describe, the pain brewed by longing

The neon lights in the night sky, are all a prosperity I do not want

Perhaps take a trip to the beach, perhaps go watch the setting sun

Perhaps any place at all where I can ponder my thoughts

Emotions in the café, are blended into an essay

Completely falling in love with your tear-glint, as transparent as a poem

Give Me the Time of One Song

Rain drenched the sky, ruining it with such exquisite care

You say you don’t understand, why I hold your hand at this moment

I dried out the silence, regretting so impulsively

Even if this is a mistake, I’m only afraid of missing out on you

Being together is called a dream, parting ways is called pain

Does that mean, an unfinished dream hurts the most

The consequence of getting lost, I can bear

This final exit, only exists after having loved

Can you give me the time of just one song

To tightly turn that embrace into forever

(Listen to the story till the very end, before saying goodbye)

In my arms, you don’t need to fear insomnia

(The tears you gave me, let them stay in the rainy day)

If you want to forget, I can lose my memory too

(Crossing the line you drew, I set the endpoint of my courage)

You say I shouldn’t, I shouldn’t

Shouldn’t have said I love you at a time like this

How can I prove I don’t even have the strength to lie

Please tell me, does pausing count as giving up

I only have one day’s worth of memories

Common Jasmin Orange

Sparrows outside the window, chatter away on the utility pole

You say this line, feels so much like summer

The pencil in my hand, goes back and forth upon the paper

I use a few lines of words to describe who you are to me

The taste of pacific saury, both the cat and you wish to know

(Those plump, ears of rice, have blessed this season with happiness)

The fragrance of first love is thus rediscovered by us

(And your cheeks are like ripe tomatoes in the field)

That warm, sunlight, is like freshly picked, vibrant strawberries

(You suddenly, tell me, that “Seven-Mile Fragrance” is a beautiful name)

You say you can’t bear to eat away this kind of feeling

(Yet at this moment, I only want to kiss your stubborn lips)

Rain falls all night, my love overflows just like the rainwater

Fallen leaves in the yard, piled thick like my longing

(Butterflies on the windowsill, like beautiful chapters fluttering in a poem)

A few words of gossip, cannot cool down my passion

(I keep on writing, writing “loving you forever” into the poem’s end)

You appear on every page of my poetry

(You are the only understanding I ever want)

Luo Tianyi

Pinky Swear

You are the beginning of a letter, the substance of a poem, the ending of a fairy tale

(You are the waypoint I seek, in my adventure across ten thousand crags and torrents)

You are a miracle taken for granted, you are “the moon is so beautiful”

(You are the line that divides my life, yet connects the pieces together again)

You are the gift Santa Claus sent to me, for being a good child

(You are the thousand backward glances in a past life, the hand held in devotion)

In the three thousand beautiful worlds, you are my single gourd of water

(You are everything else that remains, you are my world)

So let me come just a little bit closer, because you are so warm

(So please shine just a little bit brighter, even though I am so ordinary)

I will become just a little bit stronger, because you are so soft

(I will become just a little bit softer, because you are so sensitive)

Exchanging the golden contract on our ring fingers, giving each other our years

Promising to hold hands from now on, because we have a long way to go

(Promising to hold hands from now on, no matter how far we have to go)
