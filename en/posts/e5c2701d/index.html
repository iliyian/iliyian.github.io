In Elementary School, The Teacher Said The Essay Is a Special Literary Form

Created2026-01-21
|Word Count:1.4k|Reading Time:8mins|Post Views:

January 21, 2026

Yesterday, I finished the last final exam of this semester. Yesterday, rarely enough, a light snow fell; clad in silvery makeup, it was exceptionally enchanting. Although the fluttering snowflakes are hard to capture on camera even against a black background. I don’t want to go home, even though I can feel the number of people at school gradually dwindling. I still have to get a haircut first.

Many new routes taking only an hour and a half have been added from Changshan to Hangzhou, which is very good.

In the empty dormitory, the cold attacks from all directions, exploding into patches of gooseflesh upon my body. If the rupture of a relationship is an abandonment after careful consideration with words on the tip of one’s tongue, then a strand of a relationship spanning many years also seems to be teetering on the verge of breaking.

Truly regrettable.

Allow me to weep.

Actually, in the past, many people did indeed proactively express their fondness for me, but I felt as if I were my own marionette, passive, manipulated into responding: It seems I lack the ability to respond proactively. Because the fondness they expressed towards me was based on some attribute we shared. Damn it, how should this sentence be phrased? Speaking of which, I feel LR is very good at expressing such abstract things with language. Once in high school, I could only describe a certain matter as “ideological,” whereas she immediately expressed my meaning in a physical sense; it was truly impressive.

However, her appearance at that time caused me to self-heal the obvious hunchback I had maintained while standing and walking for many years. Because I thought it was ugly, and I had another very narcissistic thought, (eight characters deleted here).

This directly led to me growing much taller in height; later, during the summer vacation when I went home, many relatives felt as if I had suddenly shot up…

Anyway, it was still a very eerie thing.

It would be good if such life-changing things happened a few more times.

Speaking of the second half of last year, I sent birthday wishes to many friends: followed by small talk, but I didn’t know what to chat about. I seemed to only want to wish them a happy birthday; after all, no one feels unhappy because someone wishes them a happy birthday. But specifically chat about what? The threads of our lives are already very far apart.

It seems I have always been exploring a theme: What exactly do I crave from others? I seem to have written down discussions on this question in all sorts of places.

In the afternoon, I ordered fried chicken to serve as both lunch and dinner; this has been the coldest meal in days, as if the logistics system has frozen along with the color of the sky.

Why are there unexpectedly still so many things left unfinished?

So painful.

I feel that I have indeed learned to release kindness towards others, but when others release kindness towards me, I often do not do so.

Regarding the content written in May of last year, my current evaluation is: Every time I think I have grown, I discover I am completely marking time in the same place. She didn’t give me nearly that much. And there was absolutely no need for me to make such strange promises.

The cola tastes terrible.

May 20, 2025.

In elementary school, the teacher said the essay is a special literary form. In high school, ZXD, who loved literature, proposed submitting articles to the school newspaper together with me, who loved poetry at the time. He seemed to want to discuss the writing techniques of essays with the Chinese teacher. To this day, in the deepest part of a cabinet in my room in my hometown, I still hide a box containing many precious items from the high school period, including that one hundredth issue of the school newspaper featuring his soaring literary talent. I miss the past. I deeply miss the past. I seem to live in the past always. He always called me “Xueba” [Top Student], so much so that in Class 1, where top students gathered like clouds, I always felt a shallow shyness upon hearing it. Vscode plus markdown is indeed not as comfortable as word; my thoughts need to be spread on a sufficiently soft blanket to stretch out better.

I really want to write about the many, many things that have happened recently, linked by a thousand threads. They have had a deep influence. Someone’s silence often trades for another’s silence, because silence is simply too devoid of meaning, leaving too much space for imagined hostility; no one would take that risk. Even the person inflicting silence upon others. Language must break this. But compromising to the separation of space, words lose their attached emotions, turning into dead, unchangeable, objective text displayed on an electronic screen, which again makes one…

Just now zyx looked for me.

Just start from this break, a forced transition.

Her arrival yesterday, for me, connected my present with eight years ago. It is a rainbow bridge over the desert. It is a sudden turn of the head in the long river of time and space. But. Is there the melancholy that things are still there but people have changed?

Has she changed?

I really don’t know, nor do I hope to know, nor am I willing to imagine. I feel that apart from my emotions becoming extremely calm, I haven’t changed much. My character, my soul. Matters come tumultuously rushing towards the tip of my pen, or rather the keyboard, wanting to write themselves down. She wouldn’t be willing to listen to my complaints, nor can I bear to do so.

I want to know more about her.

Where is she? Where in Dalian? I want to fly to Dalian to find her; do I really want to? You, who are reading this sentence, who are you in my reality? What has she become like? What has her voice become like? What happened to her? Her experiences in junior high? Her experiences in high school? Her experiences in her freshman year and just recently relative to this moment? She was at Changshan No. 1 Middle School. She was certainly there too, in the same school. But why didn’t I look for her then? Do I dare to look for her now? I miss her. Who am I missing? It is the shadow of her and me in her eyes. The shadow of the past me that the present me protects with tears, and the treasure of the past me, the love of the past me. Should this word be used this way? What exactly do I want? What is her attitude towards me? I don’t know how to ask. This question is simply impossible to ask. The answer might kill all fantasies. Does she actually still care about me?

Abandon fantasy.

Still have to be a bit realistic. For my goal, to shoulder my responsibility to her under this premise. I must work hard for this reason. I know what perfection looks like. That can only be my goal.

Truly and urgently believed that love is the sinking of reason. This feeling is something I have truly never had before. It is a feeling never had with any other girl.

To give her a beautiful future, I must continue to work hard. This is my motivation.

May 21, 2025 Wednesday.
Chatted a lot with her last night. Filled many doubts and blanks. Realized many misunderstandings.

The past is gone.
If life could but remain as ’twas at first sight,
Whence would the autumn wind bring sorrow to the painted fan?
Readily do the hearts of old friends alter,
Yet ’tis said that old hearts are fickle by nature.
Talks at Lishan ceased ’midst the clear night,
Rain of tears and tolling bells, yet in the end, no spite.

Not a shred remains.

The past! The past! The past! How beautiful the past was…
I miss it.

But what exactly am I missing?
Life is gradually collapsing. It is gradually deteriorating.
From birth until now, it has always been going downhill.

There is a feeling that I am unworthy.
